Quirko — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Quirko is a mobile game published by ESM Studio (“we”, “us”). Contact: quiirko@gmail.com.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Quirko has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your progress (scores, stars, balls and settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game. The game works fully offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When your device is online, Quirko shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner at the bottom of the screen while you are in a game, optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between rounds (never during play). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where the law requires it, a consent form from Google is shown before any ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → More Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • iPhone: Apple asks whether Quirko may track you. If you decline, ads are not personalised with your IDFA.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

When your device is offline, no ads are requested and no data is sent.

Children

Quirko is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ad content is limited to the “Parental Guidance” rating.

Your rights

We do not hold any personal data about you. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK or California's CCPA), you have rights over data processed by Google for advertising. You can exercise them through Google's policy above, or write to us at quiirko@gmail.com and we will help you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
